Privacy Policy — TopE

Last updated: October 30, 2026

TopE respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we access and how we use it.

When you authorize TopE, we access your basic TikTok profile information (such as your TikTok user ID and display name) and upload only the videos you choose.

We use this data only to perform the uploads you request. We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties, except TikTok's official API to complete your uploads.

Access tokens are stored securely and are never posted publicly. We do not use analytics or tracking on this site.

You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok settings. To request deletion of your data, contact us at https://github.com/huytien812-rgb
